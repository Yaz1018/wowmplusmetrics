How to read this, and where it comes from

Built to be defensible, not to hand you one author's opinion. The numbers come from logged play; the tier blends are transparent.

Snapshot & scope

Live game state at build time: the Midnight expansion, Season 1 (slug season-mn-1, started 24 Mar 2026). The earlier The War Within seasons are closed. Throughput and high-key representation are period 1068 (week 13), dungeon keys +16 to +24. This is a snapshot, not a live feed; M+ tuning shifts weekly.

Metrics

Throughput (Avg / Top 5%) — average DPS or HPS-role damage across the whole dungeon including downtime, from the Warcraft Logs API via Mythicstats. Numbers are compressed: every DPS spec sits inside roughly a 30% band, so small gaps are not decisive.
Representation (Runs) — count of logged runs for that spec at +16–24, a clean revealed-preference signal of what high-key players actually bring.
Damage tier — Mythicstats' own six-way split of the role by average damage (one spec is always forced into F; F means lowest damage, not unplayable).
Meta tier (blended) — for DPS, a score that averages throughput rank and high-key representation rank, then bins specs into S/A/B/C/D/F. For tanks and healers the meta tier instead leans on survivability / HPS / utility from cross-source reads, because damage alone misrepresents those roles.
Opinion — a hand-made tier list (Skycoach), included only to show how far author opinion drifts from logged play.

Why not just trust a tier list?

The opinion lists openly disagree with the logs and with each other. Skycoach puts Arcane Mage and Elemental Shaman in S; in current high-key logs both sit near the bottom of the damage table. The reverse holds too: Unholy DK leads logged damage but several authors place it only A. Treat the opinion column as commentary, the logged columns as evidence, and remember that player skill and group composition move results more than any list.

Known limitations

Healer and tank "throughput" is damage only; healing and mitigation throughput are not in this feed, so those meta tiers carry more judgment. All-level popularity (e.g., Retribution at 7.4% of all M+ play) comes from an earlier-season Raider.io snapshot and is shown only as context. Sample sizes vary widely (Unholy DK 2.7K runs vs. Devastation 15), so low-run specs are noisier. Chrome rendering was unavailable at build time, so Archon and Warcraft Logs interactive pages were not scraped directly; their numbers enter through reporting and search.
