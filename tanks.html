What "success" can and cannot be measured by

You asked for percent timed, speed to timer, mitigation, damage, and more. Here is the honest data reality after going to the actual sources.

  • measured  Representation — share of logged tank runs at +12/+15/+18 (Warcraft Logs parses). The strongest revealed-preference signal: tanks that reliably succeed get brought, especially as keys climb.
  • measured  Tank damage — Warcraft Logs normalized damage score per level. A real slice of clear speed, though it barely separates tanks this season.
  • measured  Mitigation / survivability — consensus rating from current tier and mitigation reporting (Brewmaster's Stagger is the smoothest intake; Guardian's effective health is enormous; Blood DK self-heals rather than reduces; Vengeance fell after nerfs). Labeled as a rating, not a scraped number.
  • not per spec  Percent timed & speed to timer — not published per tank spec on Warcraft Logs or Raider.io, and both are driven far more by the group's total damage than by the tank. Their tank-specific signal effectively lives inside representation, so that pillar carries them.
  • not available  Damage taken, deaths, healer load — Warcraft Logs' statistics page does not expose these as per-spec aggregates for tanks (they live inside individual reports), so they are folded into the mitigation rating rather than shown as false-precision numbers.

Method & sources

Snapshot & method

Live state: Midnight expansion, Season 1, Mythic+ zone 47. Damage and representation pulled at keystone levels +12, +15, and +18 (current standings, ~2-week window), then averaged across the band. Each metric is normalized to its own top performer (value / max x 100). The aggregate is a weighted mean of the three measured pillars; weights renormalize so only their ratio matters. Mitigation is an ordinal consensus rating (0-100) derived from current tank reporting, included because raw damage-taken is not available as an aggregate. Snapshot only; tank tuning shifts weekly.

Damage & representation: Warcraft Logs zone 47 tank statistics (levels 12/15/18).
Top-end representation & trend: Mythicstats meta (Blizzard keystone leaderboard).
Mitigation / survivability consensus: Icy-Veins tank tier list, murlok.io, and aggregated tank reporting.

Snapshot for analysis only. Player skill and group composition move outcomes more than tank choice.
